Answers Before You Go Solar

Find clear answers to common questions about planning and installing a solar system.

What solar system capacity is right for my home or business?

It depends on monthly electricity usage, available roof area and site conditions. Use our calculator for an initial estimate; the final system size is confirmed after a site assessment.

Is a site inspection needed before installation?

Yes. A site assessment helps review roof condition, shade, orientation, structure and electrical requirements before a final proposal.

What maintenance does a solar system need?

Keep panels reasonably clean, monitor inverter status and arrange periodic system checks. Always follow the equipment manufacturer's maintenance guidance.

Is the calculator result a final quotation?

No. It is an initial planning estimate only. Final pricing and system design depend on the site assessment, selected equipment and project requirements.

How can I request a quotation from Riseion Solar?

Use the Contact Us form to share your details and project requirements. The team can follow up to discuss the next steps.
